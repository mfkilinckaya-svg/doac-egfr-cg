Research and teaching tool. It does not replace the product information or clinical judgement, and it ignores bleeding risk, hepatic function and other interactions.

Equations: Cockcroft–Gault 1976 (actual body weight); CKD-EPI 2009 (race term omitted); CKD-EPI 2021; EKFC (Pottel et al., Ann Intern Med 2021). Non-indexed eGFR = eGFR × BSA/1.73, BSA by DuBois. Dose rules: EU SmPC section 4.2 and US prescribing information for apixaban, dabigatran, edoxaban and rivaroxaban, stroke prevention in non-valvular AF.

Verification: the calculation code was checked against an independent Python implementation in 50 507 virtual patients, including cases placed exactly on every dose threshold, with no disagreement in any dose class, and against hand-calculated reference values. Version .
